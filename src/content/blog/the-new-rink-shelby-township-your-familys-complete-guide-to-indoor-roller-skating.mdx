## Welcome to The New Rink: Shelby Township's Premier Family Roller Skating Destination

Are you looking for a fun, safe, and affordable indoor roller skating venue in Shelby Township, Michigan? Look no further than The New Rink, your go-to destination for family-friendly roller sports. ## Discover Indoor Roller Skating Excellence

### Why Choose The New Rink?

Nestled in the heart of Shelby Township, The New Rink stands out as a community hub for roller skating enthusiasts of all ages. We pride ourselves on providing a clean, welcoming environment that caters to families, friends, and community groups. Our facility offers a wide range of [skating activities](/activities/), specialized programs, and entertainment options, making it an ideal choice for anyone searching for indoor roller skating near them.

### Our Skating Activities

At The New Rink, we offer a variety of skating disciplines to suit every preference:

- **Open Skating Sessions**: Available throughout the week, our open skating hours provide a perfect opportunity for families to enjoy time on the rink. With admissions ranging from $10 to $14 depending on the day and time, it's a budget-friendly way to stay active.

- **[Friday Family Night](/events/friday-family-fun-nights/)**: Join us every Friday evening from 7 p.m. to 10 p.m. for a special family-oriented skating event. At just $12 admission, it's a fantastic way to kick off the weekend with loved ones.

- **Saturday Night Skate**: Our Saturday evening program offers individual rates and family packages to make skating accessible for everyone. Enjoy the rink from 7 p.m. to 10 p.m. with rates of $14 per person or $50 for a family package of four.

- **[Rollers and Strollers](/activities/rollers-and-strollers/)**: Designed for children ages 7 and under, this program allows parents and toddlers to skate together in a nurturing environment. For $8, participants receive skate rentals, snacks, drinks, and access to the bounce zone.

## Equipment Rentals: Skates for Every Skater

### Find Your Perfect Pair

Choosing the right equipment is crucial for a comfortable and enjoyable skating experience. At The New Rink, we offer a variety of skate rentals to accommodate different skating styles and skill levels:

- **Roller Skate Rentals**: Our traditional four-wheel roller skates provide stability and ease for beginners and casual skaters. Available at $5 per pair, they're perfect for those new to the rink.

- **Rollerblade Rentals**: For those seeking speed and maneuverability, our in-line skates offer a thrilling ride. Rent a pair for just $5.

- **Speed Skate Rentals**: Advanced skaters looking to challenge themselves can opt for our performance speed skates, also available at $5 per pair.

- **Skate Mates Rentals**: Ideal for younger skaters or beginners, these training aids support balance and confidence on the rink. Rent them for $5 per pair.

## Instruction Programs: Learn and Grow with Confidence

### Classes for Every Skill Level

At The New Rink, we believe that everyone can develop their skating skills with the right instruction. Our experienced instructors, Sheryl Bower and Theresa Golcheff, lead classes designed to empower skaters at every level:

- **[Beginner Learn to Skate Classes](/learn-to-roller-skate-beginner-classes-at-the-new-rink-shelby-township/)**: These 5-week instructional sessions focus on building confidence and foundational skills. For $70 per session, participants receive personalized attention and encouragement.

- **Beginner Learn to Rollerblade Classes**: Designed for those new to in-line skating, this program offers a supportive environment to learn and improve. Classes are priced at $70 per session.

- **Intermediate Rollerblade/Speed Classes**: For skaters ready to advance beyond the basics, our intermediate classes provide the techniques needed to excel.
